Description

This medium-weight fleece hoodie brings quiet comfort and everyday durability together. The soft 3-end fleece exterior feels smooth against the skin while the cotton-rich face keeps prints and embroidery crisp. A roomy kangaroo pocket warms cold hands and holds essentials. The adjustable hood with self-colored drawcord and metal grommets lets you tailor coverage on blustery walks. Thoughtful construction—twill neck tape, double-needle topstitching, and a tear-away label—keeps the hoodie feeling broken-in and stable wash after wash. Available with subtle left-chest or center-chest embroidery for a clean, personalized look.

Product features
– Medium-heavy 8.25 oz fleece with soft 3-end construction
– Cotton-rich face for smooth printing; varies by heather color
– Kangaroo pouch pocket to keep hands warm
– Adjustable hood with woven drawcord and metal grommets
– Reinforced seams: twill neck tape, double-needle topstitching, tear-away label

Care instructions
– Machine wash: cold (max 30C or 90F)
– Do not bleach
– Tumble dry: low heat
– Iron, steam or dry: low heat
– Do not dryclean
